\subsubsection{Eligibility}
\label{survey:eligibility}
\begin{protocol}
\item \label{sc:req-exp} \internote{Required for eligibility, yes/no} Do you have more than 1 year of experience using Rust?

\item \label{sc:unsafe} \internote{Required for eligibility, yes/no} Do you regularly reason about the correctness of unsafe code?
\item \internote{Required for eligibility if no to previous, yes/no} Do you regularly write new Rust code or edit existing Rust code within an \unsafe block or a function?
\end{protocol}

\subsubsection{Personal Information}
\label{survey:personal-information}



The following questions request identifying information. Your affiliation will be collected so that we can determine if experiences with unsafe Rust vary based on the institutional context where development takes place. The specifics of your affiliation will not be published. Your affiliation will only be referred to in published data under broad classifications similar but not limited to ``industry,'' ``academia,'' and ``open-source.'' Your name and email are collected for scheduling and payment purposes.
\begin{protocol}
\item \label{sc:name} \internote{short answer} What is your name?
\item \label{sc:email} \internote{short answer} What is your email?
\item \label{sc:affiliation} \internote{short answer} What is your current affiliation?
\end{protocol}
